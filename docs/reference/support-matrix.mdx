| Feature | Authoring | Manifold | Stock OCCT | Owned facade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Box, cylinder/cone, sphere | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Planar sketches and holes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Extrude, symmetric extrude | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Twist/top-scale/divided extrude | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Revolve | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Ruled solid loft | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Polyline path sweep | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Circular-arc path sweep | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Composite line/arc sweep | Yes | No | Bounded | Stronger bounded refinements |
| Boolean operations | Yes | Yes | Yes, partial history | Yes, complete bounded evolution |
| Transform | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Fillet/chamfer | Yes | No | Yes, partial history | Yes, complete bounded evolution |
| Shell/whole-solid offset | Yes | No | Yes, partial history | Yes, complete bounded evolution |
| Draft | Yes | No | No | Yes |

No capability names extrude `twist`, `scaleTop`, or `divisions`. OCCT
advertises plain `extrude` and passes capability preflight, including the
`mechanical-exact` profile; a nonzero `twist`, a `scaleTop` other than
`[1, 1]`, or nonzero `divisions` then fails inside the kernel with
`KERNEL_ERROR`, not `KERNEL_CAPABILITY_MISSING`.

OCCT applies a nonuniform `scale` through a general affine transform. Results
with curved faces are approximate, so their measured volume and area can differ
from the analytic values: a cylinder scaled by `[1, 2, 1]` measures about 0.9%
too large. In published 0.1.1, every OCCT transform of a shape with a
degenerate pole or apex edge, such as a sphere or a pointed cone, fails;
unreleased 0.2 transforms them.

Unreleased 0.2 adds kernel-neutral evaluator checks that run before the
geometry kernel:

- a revolve profile must lie on one side of the revolve axis, which it may
  touch; a crossing profile fails with `FEATURE_INVALID` (reason
  `profile-crosses-axis`)
- each hole must clear its outer loop and every other hole by more than the
  sketch tolerance; touching, crossing, outside, or nested holes fail with
  `SKETCH_NO_CLOSED_REGION`
- each straight sweep-path segment must be longer than the right-corner miter
  setbacks at its ends, and remote segments must clear the outer miter reach;
  otherwise the sweep fails with `FEATURE_INVALID` (reason `path-clearance`).
  See [clearance and conditioning](/modeling/paths-sweeps-and-lofts#clearance-and-conditioning).

## Topology

| Capability | Manifold | Stock OCCT | Owned facade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Face/edge/vertex snapshot | No | Yes | Yes |
| Semantic roles and sketch source | No | Yes, feature-dependent | Yes, feature-dependent |
| Geometry and adjacency queries | No | Yes | Yes |
| Selection explanations | Snapshot-dependent | Yes | Yes |
| Persistent signature protocol v2 | No | Bundled runtime only | Yes |
| Stored v1 compatibility profile | No | Bundled runtime only | Yes |
| Complete feature evolution | No | Partial | Selected feature slices |

Construction and complete history are separate columns conceptually. A stock
OCCT fillet may construct successfully while downstream semantic lineage is
partial.

Stock OCCT advertises both signature rows only from the bundled runtime that
`createOcctKernel()` loads when neither `wasm` nor `moduleFactory` is supplied.
An explicit `wasm` or `moduleFactory` without the owned facade ABI omits both,
even with byte-identical stock files. The `mechanical-exact` preflight still
passes, but a document with persistent topology references then fails with
`KERNEL_CAPABILITY_MISSING`.

## Product structure and analysis

These evaluator-level features work with either kernel when all underlying
geometry features are supported:

- parameters and dimension-safe expressions
- materials and density expressions
- parts and fixed-placement nested assemblies
- named configurations
- instance suppression and part-material overrides
- deterministic BOMs
- geometric and physical mass properties
- canonical documents, migration, validation, and hashing
- design-impact and effective-feature hash reports

| Measurement | Manifold | Stock OCCT | Owned facade | Assembly aggregate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Volume, area, bounds, center, inertia | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Genus (unreleased 0.2) | Exact per connected mesh component | Unsupported (`null`) | Unsupported (`null`) | Unsupported (`null`) |

<Note>
  The nullable genus contract is unreleased 0.2. Published 0.1.1 types genus
  as `number` and always returns one: OCCT derives a heuristic integer from the
  whole shape's Euler characteristic, clamped at zero; Manifold reports its
  whole-mesh genus, which is negative for disjoint bodies; and assembly
  aggregates report `0`.
</Note>

In unreleased 0.2, a numeric genus is exact for the backend representation.
Unsupported results are explicit `null`; no backend or assembly path guesses,
clamps, or silently converts the value.

<Note>
  Body-result classification is implemented for the unreleased 0.2 line, not
  the published 0.1.1 package.
</Note>

| Body-result classification | Manifold | Stock OCCT | Owned facade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basis | Positive-volume connected components of the approximate mesh | Exact compound/compsolid solid occurrences | Same exact traversal as stock OCCT |
| Overlapping union, cavity subtraction | `single-body` | `single-body` | Not separately release-gated |
| Disjoint union, split subtraction | `multiple-bodies` | `multiple-bodies` | Not separately release-gated |
| Empty result | `non-body-topology` | `non-body-topology` | Not separately release-gated |
| Solid beside a loose face, or face only | Not representable | `non-body-topology` | Not separately release-gated |

The repository-staged Document v7 evaluator admits a Boolean result only when
its kernel classifies it as `single-body`; explicit body sets remain the only
multibody product boundary.

| --- | --- | --- |

<Note>
  The strong deterministic STEP paragraph and verified-import table below
  describe the unreleased 0.2 line. The exchange table above remains the
  published 0.1.1 boundary.
</Note>

The bundled zero-override stock OCCT runtime additionally advertises
single-product deterministic STEP export protocol v1: identical bytes require
the same backend shape representation, resolved metadata/options,
implementation, and exact runtime artifact. Explicit `wasm`, `moduleFactory`,
and attested runtimes retain weak raw STEP and omit this envelope until
separately qualified. An output that OCCT writes as several STEP products, such
as a disjoint union, returns weak native bytes from a plain `export("step")`
and fails with `EXPORT_UNSUPPORTED` (reason `multiple-products`) when STEP
options are passed. This is narrower than geometric canonicalization and
does not make STEP a portable artifact or cache format. Exact aggregate
body-set and assembly STEP remains unsupported.

The unreleased public imported-body workflow admits exactly one committed
solid through stock OCCT's strong document-body import capability:

| Imported format | Required media type | Unit policy | Manifold | Stock OCCT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STEP | `model/step` | File units | No | Yes |
| Text BREP | `text/plain` | Declared `mm`, `cm`, `m`, or `in` | No | Yes |
| Binary BREP | `application/octet-stream` | Declared `mm`, `cm`, `m`, or `in` | No | Yes |

This is a separate opaque one-body document and
`Evaluator.evaluateImportedBody(...)` method. It verifies a caller-supplied
SHA-256 and byte-length commitment before parsing, fixes healing to `none`, and
does not publish the broader Document v7 product model. Its JSON is a frozen,
minimal [Document v7 subset](/interchange/import-export#imported-body-document-format)
(schema `https://invariantcad.dev/schema/document/v7`, version `7`) that a
future public Document v7 will keep accepting. Only the imported-body functions
read, write, and evaluate it: `parseDocument`, `validateDocument`, and the other
general v1-v6 document APIs return `IR_INVALID` at `/`, and
`stringifyDocument`, `hashDocument`, and `cloneDocument` throw `TypeError`.
Multibody import, assembly import, repair/healing, automatic file/URL I/O, and
approximate fallback remain unsupported.

## Explicitly not in 0.1

- drawings, dimensions, PMI, and GD&T
- sheet metal and flat patterns
- assembly mates, joints, motion, interference, and contact
- CAM, toolpaths, posts, and stock simulation
- CAE/meshing/solver workflows
- general surfacing, NURBS editing, healing, and feature recognition
- variable fillets/chamfers and advanced shell modes
- complete transparent cross-run native shape caching

For the exact path-conditioning and topology-protocol boundaries behind this
matrix, read the [complete guide](/reference/complete-guide).
